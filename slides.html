The Problem: High Barrier to Earth Data

While NASA collects petabytes of atmospheric and oceanic data, general audiences rarely interact with raw telemetry. Scientific diagrams, cloud-top infrared temperatures, and barometric curves remain abstract and disconnected from sensory experience.

Our Solution: Real-Time Data Sonification

Cyclone Pulse bridges Earth observation and musical rhythm. Players navigate genuine historical storm paths across Taiwan, feeling the intensity of wind shear through tempo changes and pressure through bass depth, making planetary science directly audible.

Key Innovation Highlights
  • 🌪️ 10 Real Taiwan Typhoons: Includes exact landfall timestamps (YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm), coordinates, and true NASA satellite clouds.
  • 🎵 4 Spatial Performance Instruments: Players freely play Celeste Bell, Warm Rhodes, Kalimba, or Analog Strings.
  • 🎧 Automated Personal .WAV Export: Offline synthesis renders a unique soundtrack combining storm telemetry and the user's performance.

Data-Driven Sonification & Phase-Locked Rhythm
04 / 07
💨 Wind Speed (m/s) ➔ Tempo (68–172 RPM)

Wind speed dynamically regulates musical tempo and storm motion. Outer bands (18 m/s) glide at 0.4x speed / 76 RPM, while peak landfall (55 m/s) sprints at 2.4x speed / 168 RPM with high-tension 16th-note shaker rhythms.

🌀 Central Pressure (hPa) ➔ Sub-Bass Resonance

Deep low-pressure centers (down to 905 hPa) directly modulate low-pass filter cutoff frequencies, driving resonant analog sub-bass drones and stereo reverb.

⏱️ 4/4 Phase-Locked Master Clock

A continuous musical clock synchronizes audio and canvas frames. Each 4/4 bar features four structured beats:

Beat 1 (Downbeat): Heavy Kick + Sub-Bass + Bell
Beat 2 (Backbeat): Crisp Snare / Rim Clap
Beat 3 (Driving): Double Kick Propulsion
Beat 4 (Turnaround): 16th-note Fill

Audio Synthesis Engine
Offline Audio Synthesis & Personalized .WAV Export
06 / 07
Automated OfflineAudioContext Pipeline

When a stage concludes, the system triggers a background OfflineAudioContext pipeline (44.1 kHz, 16-bit stereo) that calculates the exact meteorological trajectory alongside the player's recorded instrument inputs.

Zero Identical Tracks

Because each typhoon has unique pressure and wind profiles, and every player chooses different directional instruments, every generated track is a personalized piece of meteorological music.

Studio-Grade Audio Features
  • 🎛️ Dynamics Compressor: Tight punchy master bus dynamics (-12 dB threshold, 4:1 ratio).
  • 🏛️ Impulse Convolver Reverb: 1.8-second simulated hall acoustic space.
  • 🎚️ Stereo Ping-Pong Delay: 250ms left / 375ms right tempo-synced delay tails.
  • 💾 Lossless .WAV Generator: Instant in-browser binary WAV encoding with one-click download.
